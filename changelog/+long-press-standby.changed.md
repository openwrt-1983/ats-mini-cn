A long press now enters deep sleep standby instead of only blanking the screen: the display, amplifier, receiver and network links are powered down and the CPU stops, leaving the encoder button as the only wake source. Holding the button for the same two seconds wakes the receiver, which restarts, sets the SI4732 up again and resumes audio on the stored band and volume without showing the boot splash. Boards where the encoder button doubles as BOOT keep the display-only sleep, because holding it at reset enters the ROM download mode.
