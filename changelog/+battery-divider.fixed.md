The divider factor is the nominal 2.0 instead of the 1.83 calibrated on the original board. Clone boards use an exact 100k/100k divider, where 1.83 read every voltage about 8.5% low: the USB rail (~4.4 V) fell below the 4.3 V charging threshold, so the charging icon never appeared, and a 3.7 V cell was displayed as 3.42 V. With the correct factor the charging icon shows while plugged in and the displayed cell voltage matches a meter. The first reading after the USB cable is pulled now snaps the fast filter, the slow average and the charge level to the real cell voltage, instead of the voltage text decaying from the rail for a few seconds while the bars walk down from 100% level by level.
