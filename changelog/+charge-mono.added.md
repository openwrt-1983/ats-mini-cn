Settings gained a "充电单声道" (mono while charging) switch, on by default. While a USB supply is connected the receiver pins its stereo blend thresholds out of range, so the FM decoder stops turning the pilot and subcarrier noise into audible hiss; pulling the cable restores the stereo setting the user picked. The detection reuses the battery monitor's USB test and has to hold for 600 ms before it is applied, because the rail sits only ~130 mV above the threshold and a load burst must not flip the decoder back and forth. The stereo indicator stays dark while the override is active, and the switch is stored with the other settings and travels in backups.
