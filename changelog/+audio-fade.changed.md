Mute, unmute, sleep transitions, seeking and power-on now fade the volume in small steps instead of jumping it in one write, so the amplifier no longer reproduces the level change as an audible thump. The backlight PWM frequency was raised from 16 kHz to 20 kHz to keep its switching out of the audible range, and the headphone clamp is released once the volume has faded in after power-on. The fade runs on the main loop, so it was also shortened: a seek starts one per encoder detent, and the original pace spent about 40 ms per transition and swallowed detents turned during it.
