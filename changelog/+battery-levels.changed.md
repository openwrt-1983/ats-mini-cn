The battery bar now has nine levels instead of four, so it moves in ~10% steps instead of jumping by a quarter of the charge at a time. The nine thresholds follow a Li-ion discharge curve and keep the previous 25/50/75% calibration points, and the hysteresis band (30 mV) is narrow enough to stay clear of them, so a cell sitting on a boundary no longer makes the bar chatter.

Every measurement now averages nine samples: fifteen conversions are taken, the first two are burnt after the channel switch, and the two lowest and two highest of the remaining thirteen are dropped. Three problems that pulled the reading away were fixed as well. A burst that produced too few samples returned the filtered cell voltage instead of a pin voltage, mixing the two scales. The first sample after the USB cable was pulled fed the USB rail voltage into the slow state-of-charge filter and pushed the bar up by a few tens of millivolts. And the ADC calibration table is built from the global attenuation, not from the per-pin one, so both are now set explicitly.
