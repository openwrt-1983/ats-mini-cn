Settings gained an "Encoder" item so the decoder can be matched to the mechanical encoder in use without going through the Wi-Fi configuration page. Two positions are offered, both mapping one mechanical detent to one tuning step: "30 detents / 15 pulses" for the stock encoder (half-step decoding, the previous default) and "20 detents / 20 pulses" for common EC11 types (full-step decoding). Changing it takes effect immediately, is written to preferences, and resets any pending encoder counts so the display does not jump.
