The battery scale factor did not match the hardware: the divider is the nominal 100k/100k, but the ADC reads it about 9.5% low because the divider presents ~50k of source impedance and the ESP32-S3 sample-and-hold settles short of the real value. A cell that a meter shows at 4.20 V right after a full charge was displayed as 3.80 V, and the USB rail fell below the 4.30 V charging threshold, so the charging icon never appeared. The factor is now 2.21 (2.00 x 4.20/3.80) and the charging threshold moved to 4.40 V, which sits halfway between a full cell (4.20 V) and the supply rail (~4.89 V) instead of only 100 mV above a full cell. The first reading after the USB cable is pulled snaps the fast filter, the slow average and the charge level to the real cell voltage, so the text no longer decays from the rail for a few seconds while the bars walk down from 100% level by level.
